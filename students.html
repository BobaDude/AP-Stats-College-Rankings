Student Population

Number of Students (Student Population) was selected because school size can shape a student’s overall experience. Larger schools often provide more opportunities through clubs, majors, research programs, and campus activities, while smaller schools may offer more individualized attention and smaller class sizes. Although there is no universally “better” school size, I included this category because institutional scale can influence available resources and overall campus experience.

Statistical Summary: Student Population

The average student population is about 14,236 students. This means most schools in this dataset are medium to large sized universities.

The standard deviation is really high at about 13,155, which shows that there is a huge difference in school sizes. Some schools are really small like Kenyon, while others like UCLA are extremely large.
