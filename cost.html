Annual Cost

Annual Cost (Tuition + Average Cost of Attendance) was chosen because affordability plays a major role in college decisions for many students and families. While some colleges may offer strong academics, their cost can make attendance unrealistic or create significant financial burden after graduation. I included this category to recognize that value matters alongside reputation. Colleges with lower overall costs were rewarded because they potentially offer a more accessible path to higher education.

Annual Cost: Z-Score & Percentile

UVA has an annual cost of about $22,000. The z-score is around -0.9, which means it is below the average cost.
