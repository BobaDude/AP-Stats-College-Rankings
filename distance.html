Distance From Home

Distance From Home was included because proximity is often an important practical consideration for students. Colleges closer to home may reduce travel costs, make visiting family easier, and create a smoother transition into college life. At the same time, schools farther away may provide more independence and new experiences. Since distance impacts both financial and personal factors, I believed it should contribute to the overall ranking. All that said I would perfer to stay close to home.

Statistical Summary: Distance From Cambridge

The average distance from Cambridge is about 642.88 miles. This means most of the colleges are not super close, but also not all across the country.

The standard deviation is about 619.78 miles, which is pretty large. This makes sense because some schools are nearby like Dartmouth, while others like UCLA are really far away.
