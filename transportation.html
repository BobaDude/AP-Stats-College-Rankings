Public Transportation Accessibility

Public Transportation Accessibility was included because transportation can significantly affect student life and convenience. Colleges located in areas with strong public transportation systems may provide students with easier access to internships, jobs, entertainment, and travel without requiring a personal vehicle. I believed this category was useful because accessibility influences both quality of life and independence, especially for students coming from outside the immediate area.
