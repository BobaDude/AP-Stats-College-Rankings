Final Weighting System

After selecting these categories, I assigned different weights to reflect their relative importance in my decision-making process. Categories connected to long-term outcomes and affordability, such as Graduation Rate and Annual Cost, received greater emphasis, while categories such as transportation, student population, and distance contributed less to the final score. This allowed me to create a ranking system that reflected both objective statistics and factors that I will realistically consider when evaluating colleges.
